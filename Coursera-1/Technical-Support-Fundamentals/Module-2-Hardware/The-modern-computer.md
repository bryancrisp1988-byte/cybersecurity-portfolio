# The Modern Computer — Module 2: Computer Hardware

## What This Lesson Covers
This lesson examines how modern computers have evolved — from early machines to today’s multi-core, cloud-connected systems.

## Key Learning Points
- Evolution from mechanical to digital computing.
- Miniaturization and efficiency improvements.
- Integration of GPUs, SSDs, and wireless connectivity.
- The role of virtualization and cloud computing.

## Why It Matters
Modern hardware design influences performance, energy use, and scalability — critical for IT support and cybersecurity.
