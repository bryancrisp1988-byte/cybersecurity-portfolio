# Customer Service — Module 6: Troubleshooting

## What This Lesson Covers
This lesson explores how effective communication and empathy are essential when helping users resolve technical issues.

## Key Learning Points
- Active listening helps identify the real problem.
- Clear, jargon‑free explanations build trust.
- Empathy and patience improve user experience.
- Professional tone and documentation maintain credibility.

## Why It Matters
Strong customer service skills ensure technical solutions are delivered effectively and respectfully, strengthening relationships and reputation.
