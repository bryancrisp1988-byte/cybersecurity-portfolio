# Troubleshooting Best Practices — Module 6: Troubleshooting

## What This Lesson Covers
This lesson highlights proven methods and habits that make troubleshooting faster, more accurate, and more reliable.

## Key Learning Points
- Follow a logical, repeatable process.
- Use diagnostic tools effectively.
- Keep calm and avoid assumptions.
- Collaborate when issues span multiple systems.

## Why It Matters
Best practices ensure consistency, reduce stress, and improve success rates when resolving complex technical problems.
