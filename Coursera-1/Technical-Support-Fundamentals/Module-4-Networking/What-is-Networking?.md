# What Is Networking? — Module 4: Networking

## What This Lesson Covers
This lesson introduces the concept of computer networking — how devices communicate and share data through wired and wireless connections.

## Key Learning Points
- Networking connects computers, servers, and devices to exchange information.
- Networks can be local (LAN), wide (WAN), or global (Internet).
- Communication relies on protocols such as TCP/IP.
- Routers, switches, and access points manage data flow.

## Why It Matters
Networking forms the backbone of modern computing, enabling collaboration, cloud access, and cybersecurity monitoring.
