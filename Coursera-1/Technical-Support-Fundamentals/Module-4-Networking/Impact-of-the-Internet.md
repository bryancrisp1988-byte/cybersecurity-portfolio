# Impact of the Internet — Module 4: Networking

## What This Lesson Covers
This lesson explores how the Internet transformed communication, business, and society by connecting billions of devices worldwide.

## Key Learning Points
- The Internet enables instant global communication.
- It drives innovation in education, commerce, and entertainment.
- Cloud computing and IoT rely on Internet connectivity.
- Cybersecurity threats increase with global connectivity.

## Why It Matters
Understanding the Internet’s impact helps IT professionals balance accessibility with security and privacy.
