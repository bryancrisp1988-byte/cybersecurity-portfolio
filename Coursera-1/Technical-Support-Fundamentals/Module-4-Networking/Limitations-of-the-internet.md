# Limitations of the Internet — Module 4: Networking

## What This Lesson Covers
This lesson examines the challenges and limitations of Internet technology, including bandwidth, reliability, and security concerns.

## Key Learning Points
- Network congestion can slow data transfer.
- Connectivity depends on infrastructure and geography.
- Privacy and data protection remain ongoing challenges.
- Cyberattacks exploit weaknesses in Internet protocols.

## Why It Matters
Recognising Internet limitations helps IT professionals design secure, efficient networks and mitigate risks.
